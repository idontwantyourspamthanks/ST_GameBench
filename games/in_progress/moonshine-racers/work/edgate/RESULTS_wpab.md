**Findings the lead / the user must see (not code faults, a consequence of the decided rule) [obs + INFERENCE]:**
1. The un-upgraded car does about 10 units per pass at full throttle (`$5ff8` 1956 after 250 passes = 55 lights + 196 at 10 / pass; `hud.md`: speed saturates at `$5c`). The rule "scenery's own pace + 100 passes" asks 12.6 units/pass beyond the first 100 passes on level 0 (and 13.3 on level 4). So: a 1000-unit road is easy (148 of 255 passes), a **2000-unit road on scenery 1 is on the edge: 250 / 257 of 258 passes used with a perfect straight run, 1 pass to spare in the mixed run, a human will time out**; a 6000-unit road cannot be finished by the un-upgraded car (time-out at `$5ff8` 4768 of 6000 on level 0, 3852 on level 4 with crashes). The stock game has the same property for its own 19472 units [INFERENCE: not run here; 1593 passes x 10 = 15.9k < 19.5k], so it relies on the shop's upgrades; a campaign track on a high scenery from race 1 has no upgrades. If the user wants hand-built roads to be winnable at race 1, the start allowance P0 (100) and/or the pace need to change (e.g. P = 55 + L/9 + margin); `cp_len` is 3 constants (`#100`, `#100`, `$ff0000`), the rule is in one place.
2. 255-pass floor reached for L < about 1950 on scenery 1 (1000 -> floor).
