Populous 2 (Atari ST) - patched FLOPPY build, {DATE}
Release v@VERSION@ enhanced by dad_prg

One bootable 720K disk with the whole game: the patched RUNME in AUTO\ (TOS
runs it at boot), HAGA, the STE cover picture and FILES\ (the game data).
{PATCHES}

DISK LAYOUT
  AUTO\RUNME.PRG   the game's start program (PP's RUNME.TOS, patched)
  HAGA             PP's disk service
  FP2273.HST       cover picture (STE hi-colour; a plain ST or TT/Falcon
                   simply skips a picture it cannot open)
  FILES\           P2CR.TOS, P2D, P2M.FIC - the game

STARTING
  Put the disk in drive A and reset (or run A:\AUTO\RUNME.PRG). Hold Space
  through PP's hardware screen, the cover, the logo and the credits; the
  main menu follows (it shows "v@VERSION@ enhanced by dad_prg").

MEMORY
  2 MB RAM minimum (PP's conversion; 1 MB does not work).

SAVING
  HAGA's keypad / statesave writes about 1 MB and does not fit on a floppy.
  PP's conversion does not support the game's own SAVE GAME (it crashes even
  unpatched, with no disk in the drive). Keep a hard-disk install for saving.
