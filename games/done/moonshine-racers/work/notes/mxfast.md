## 0. Result in one page
* **The viewcrop finding is confirmed in Hatari [M].** Per tick with one extra at 8 MHz (race_drive, `X_TEST`=1, Hatari's per-address cycle profile, section 1): mx_sync costs **94.7 k cycles per group + 13.4 k for `mx_pre`'s copy = 108 k (0.675 VBL)**: 36.1 k `saveundo` + 40.2 k `restoreundo` + 13.0 k per extra slot reset + 5.3 k glue/ticker/cars.
* **`mf_sync` (+ `mf_pre`) = the same behaviour, code only, bit-exact [M]:** 61.4 k per tick with an extra (0.383 VBL, **-46.7 k**). Every flip (main + extras) pixel-identical to mx_sync and the whole RAM after each group identical (21 scenes x 300-800 flips in the exact Unicorn model, 0 differing frames, 0 differing addresses), per-pass state equal to stock at 16/32 MHz in Hatari.
* **`mg_sync` + `mg_pace` = the extra frames are drawn BEFORE the post stages of the pass (collision/debris logic, `$18888 $18c86 $18f0e`) [M]:** the main frame flips earlier, no pre-draw slot copy (`mx_pre` gone), only the slots that are active are saved: **28.1 k per tick with an extra (0.175 VBL, -80 k = -0.5 VBL)**, main frames and per-pass state identical, extras identical to mx_sync's **except** where the post stages change what an extra reads (the HUD strip in crash frames, the lateral position in a collision bounce): 0 of 300-600 flips in the start scene, 2 of 445 on a normal run, 12 of 600 / 42 of 600 on collision-heavy runs, 188 of 10,594 over 21 scenes (section 5.3). This deviation needs the user's say; `mf_sync` has none.
* **Share of racing ticks with an extra at 8 MHz, steered 2-minute driving, tick ON (section 7) [M]:** mx_sync (default today) **28.8 %** (12.73 fps displayed) -> `mf_sync` 33.3 % (13.14) -> `mg_sync` **57.6 %** (15.48). Raising the deadline margin (run-time word `SAFEX`) trades late ticks for more extras: mg_sync 62.7 % / 66.1 % (15.98 / 16.45 fps) at margin 3 / 6 clock units (0.5 / 1 VBL) with 18 % / 26 % of the ticks ending > 5 VBL.
* **~20 fps (an extra in every tick) is NOT reachable on the 8 MHz STE with any machinery change [S][M]:** the main pass is 2.9 VBL, an extra frame 2.4 VBL (sprites 186 k, road draw 101 k, geometry 57 k, horizon 33 k cycles, section 9) of a 5 VBL tick = 5.3-5.5 VBL even with zero overhead; the best measured share is 66 % (16.45 fps) with half of the extras ending late. The packing bound (utilisation) is about 0.78 extras per tick with mg_sync's cost [S, section 6].
* **A finer clock (video counter / Timer C) does not help [S][M]:** Timer C (`$4ba`) is frozen in the game (read at 8 pass starts: 6048 every time); a simulation with the measured per-tick data shows that exact time or even a per-tick duration prediction (corr 0.91 between the main pass length and the extra's) gives the same share/lateness curve as the VBL-floor clock; the knob that matters is the margin (section 6).

## 2. What is saved and restored, and why [D][M]
Group = the extras of one tick; the first extra saves the state (`saveundo`), the group end puts it back (`restoreundo`); every extra first puts the object slots back to what the main frame's draw saw.
* **Static ranges (118 B):** `$10ec 14` face state, `$12f8 6 $1308 4` horizon/stripe phase, `$1316 16 $13a2 16 $1bb0 12 $23bc 12 $27c8 38` generator states/heads (all advanced by `fe50 ff76 1003c 100b4 10a98`).
* **Ring windows:** `fe50` (rings `$13b0`/`$17b0`, head `$13ae`), `ff76` (curve ring `$1bbc`, head `$1bba`, 2 bytes per sample), `1003c` (hill ring `$23c8`, head `$23c6`) write ONE sample per world unit from the head (`work/extrap/dis/1003c.txt`, `$fe50`, `$ff76`: `move.b d4,(a0,d6.w)`, `move.w d3,(a0,d1.w)`); an extra advances by `k = KNEW-KCUM <= V` (speed of the tick, <= 31) and the group by <= V samples in all. mx saved fixed 32/32/64/32-byte windows byte-wise (28 cycles/byte); **V (V / V / 2V / V) bytes are enough**, and aligned longs do it (rings are 4-aligned, sizes multiples of 4, so no long straddles the wrap).
* **48 object slots `$2c68-$36e7`:** the extra needs the state the draw saw. `e406` writes one field of the slots (the y word `$2(a2)`: `sub.w d6,2(a2)` at `$e612 $e66a $ecec`, PC census in `e406_writes.py`); the post stages `$18888 $18c86 $18f0e` write other fields (byte 5 of the cars every pass, whole state machines in crash/effect events). The write census of the extras (`slots_trace2.py`): slot offsets 0-5 (x, y, flags), `$a`, `$12`, `$20`, `$2c`, `$32`; every stage an extra runs (`tc_dist` `$b6a00`, `carstep`, `tc_proj` `$b6c00`, `tc_ord`/`e598`/`e568`) tests bit 0 of byte 4 first (`work/extrap/dis/e598.txt`, `tc_dist.s`, `tc_proj.s`, `tc_ord.s`), only 19-35 of the 48 slots are active (`actcount.py`: start 23.5, up 25.7, L0 23.2, L3 32.1, L5 23.3, L7 19.3, L10 27.7, L12 26.2, L14 23.5 on average).
* **100 self-modified kernel longs (`KTAB`) + `$dafe`:** 39 of 101 change in the runs of `ktab_changes.py` (start/L3/L10); the stock quirk "a sprite uses the immediate patched by the previous draw" makes the next main frame depend on them.
* **Redundant in mx_sync:** the second 2,688-byte copy per extra (`prerestore`) and the pre-draw copy of the whole slot array although at most half the slots are active; byte-wise/word-wise loops; fixed 160-byte ring windows; a table walk for 101 constant addresses; the `wantnext` prediction (only needed because `mx_pre` copies; it costs ~0.1-0.2 k and is gone in mg).
* **What the post stages read:** `residue_vs_post.py` (emulator, 40-80 passes x 4 scenes): addresses an extras group leaves different (display scratch: order list `$36e8-$3717`, `$3732..`, `$37c0..`, road geometry output `$4724-$4f77`, raster scratch, buffer pointers) vs addresses `$7b52-$7b74` (the post stages) read: **0 in common** in all four scenes, which is what lets mg_sync run the extras first.

## 3. The two designs
### 3.1 `mf_sync` + `mf_pre` (exact drop-in) [D]
Same site `$7b74`, same blob/data addresses as mx_sync; the `extras:` scheduler block differs from mx_sync's by the three `SAFEX` lines only (`difflib`) (declared `conflicts: mt_sync mx_sync mx_pre`); `mf_pre` replaces `mx_pre` (`mx_pre` says `requires: mx_sync`, so it cannot be used with `mf_sync`; either `mx_pre.s` needs `requires: mx_sync|mf_sync`-style relaxation, which the tool does not have, or the two stay separate patches). Changes: static ranges as `movem.l` straight code (28 longs + 3 words); rings V bytes as aligned longs; **slots as unrolled 12-register `movem.l` bursts, `(a0)+` loads and `-(a1)` stores (212 cycles per 48 bytes = 4.4 per byte, 11.9 k per 2,688 B)**; kernel longs `move.l d16(a0),(a1)+` / `move.l (a0)+,d16(a1)` from base `$12000` (20 cycles per long, straight code, `$dafe` separately); `mf_pre` without the register save (the next instruction is `jmp $e406`, `tc_ord` loads everything it uses); ticker state copies as `movem.l` (7 longs; the `hud:` call list is unchanged). Layout: UNDO `$B9000` static +0..117, `U_SLOT` +`$C0` (written downwards), `U_WIN` +`$B40` (packed), `U_KERN` +`$BF0`. Code ends at `$B8DE2` (limit `$B8F00`). New run-time word `SAFEX` `$B8F6C` (signed margin in clock units added to `SAFE`; 0 = mx_sync's rule).
### 3.2 `mg_sync` + `mg_pace`: draw the extras first [D][M]
* **Site `$7b52`** (`tst.b $1024.w / bne.b $7b5e`, the first instruction after the HUD stage, before the post stages; `orig 4a3810246606`) -> `jsr $B8100.l`. With `$1024` = 0 the blob flips the main frame (the flip the pacer does at `$7b74` in mx), runs the group (scheduler, rings, road, objects, HUD list of mx_sync unchanged), restores, and returns to `$7b58` (the `jsr $18888`); with `$1024` != 0 it does the stock `bne` itself (`addq.l #6,(a7)`: return to `$7b5e`). `$1024` is final at `$7b52` (all writers are earlier stages: `$fa62 $fad6 $fb36 $10d0a $116e6 $117e4` in `work/hud/dis/all_7000_19000.txt`; the pass has no screen write after `$7b52`: `post_trace.py`, 0 writes in the screen buffers over 4 scenes/100-150 passes). `mg_pace` (site `$7b74`, `$B8180`): flips only when `FLIPPED` is clear (non-racing passes), then mx_sync's unchanged sleep (CLK/NEXT/TICK/MAXLAG). The tick grid and `mt_clock`/`mt_pre` are untouched.
* **No `mx_pre`**: at `$7b52` the slots hold the state the draw left (P' = P + the y word `e406` wrote, which `tc_proj` rewrites for every visible slot before `e406` reads it). **Only the slots active at the group start are saved** (bit 0 of `$4`, list of word addresses `U_LIST` `$B9E00`) and only the fields the extras' stages write (bytes 0-19, `$20-$23`, `$2c-$33`: 32 of 56 bytes): 8.4-11 k instead of 33.6 k. The first extra needs no slot reset (`slotreset` only from the second extra on), the group end restores the list; everything an extra can change is in an active slot (each stage tests bit 0 first).
* The post stages then run on the restored state: the per-pass state at `$7b86` equals stock (state gates below). The post stages run after the extras instead of before the flip (they cost ~6 k, `RSN` unchanged).
* What mg_sync draws differently from mx_sync: mx runs the extras AFTER the post stages (state S), mg before them (state P'). The slots are the same in both (mx restores P). The globals differ only where a post stage changes something an extra reads: **HUD strip rows 1-14** (`$10d8` speed after a crash slowdown, `$11ce` alarm, thermometer cache; in mg the extra's strip equals the main frame's strip, in mx it shows the next step) and the **lateral position `$1318`** in a collision bounce (mx's extras start from the bounced position and the main frame then shows the same bounce one pass later; mg's extras start from the main frame's position; the pass after also differs because `LATP`/`DLAT` carry the history). Section 5.3 counts them.

## 6. The scheduler [S][M]
The question (MSRTripleStudy): the clock is VBL-granular, 166 of 320 extras flip after the 5 VBL grid, 61 ticks end >= 1 VBL late, after a late tick only 3 % get an extra, the packing bound is ~0.70 vs 0.29 measured.
* Timer C (`$4ba`, 200 Hz): frozen in the race (`clk_probe.py`: 8 samples at the pass top all read `6048`), so it is not a clock here. The video counter (`$ff8205-09`) exists and is readable (`$070f00`-style values; base `$ff8201/03`), but it only resolves the 200 display lines of a 313-line frame and nothing in the two blanking intervals (36 % of the frame), and its geometry differs by machine/refresh (50/60/71 Hz, Falcon/TT video modes) [I].
* **Simulation of the packing problem** (`sched_sim.py`, `sched_sim2.py`; the measured per-tick data of MSRTripleStudy: 1,299 main pass lengths, mean 2.92 VBL; 345 extra costs, mean 2.96 VBL incl. undo; queue model: a late extra delays the next pass, the grid does not drift, resync after 2 ticks): at equal share the coarse (VBL-floor) rule, an exact-time rule and an exact-time rule with a per-tick duration prediction `D = 0.52 VBL + 0.95 m` (corr 0.91, residual sd 0.12 VBL) give the SAME lateness (extras/tick 0.52-0.53: lateness mean 0.14-0.15, p90 0.55-0.57, p99 0.83-0.91 VBL in all three). **The share/lateness trade-off is set by the utilisation (main 2.92 VBL + extra 2.5 VBL > 5 VBL), not by the clock resolution.** A finer clock only turns the coarse rule's steps (share jumps 0.53 -> 0.72 between margins) into a smooth knob.
* So the knob is the margin, now a run-time word `SAFEX` (`$B8F6C`, signed clock units, + = stricter, - = an extra may end that much after the tick is due; the tick grid stays exact: passes 10.00/s in every run). Hatari, 8 MHz, steered, 1,113 racing ticks each (`ext_sum2.py`; `>5` = share of ticks whose next pass started more than 5 VBL later, `>=7` = 2+ VBL late):
