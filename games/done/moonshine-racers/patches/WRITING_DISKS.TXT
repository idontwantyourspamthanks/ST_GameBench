Moonshine Racers v@VERSION@ enhanced by dad_prg - writing the two disks to real floppies
=========================================================================================

THE FILES
  disk_a.st / disk_a.msa   Disk 1 (boot disk - boot from this one)
  disk_b.st / disk_b.msa   Disk 2 (data disk - swap into A: at the prompt, or keep in B:)

The .st and .msa files of each disk hold the exact same 800 KB of sector data
(80 tracks x 2 sides x 10 sectors of 512 bytes = 819,200 bytes).  The .msa is an
uncompressed Magic Shadow Archiver wrapper whose header explicitly declares the
80 x 2 x 10 geometry so disk writers do not have to guess it.  Use whichever
format your writer prefers.

IMPORTANT: 800 KB (10 SECTORS PER TRACK) ON DOUBLE-DENSITY MEDIA
* Media: 720 KB double-density (DD) double-sided 3.5" disks (not 1.44 MB HD disks).
  Both disks are formatted to 80 tracks x 2 sides x 10 sectors per track (800 KB),
  the exact format of the original Millennium release.
* Because there are 10 sectors per track instead of 9, the writer MUST format/write
  the disk as 80 x 2 x 10 (not standard 720 KB 80 x 2 x 9).

WHAT v1.4.2 FIXED (Makedisk "Divide Error" on Disk 1)
* In v1.0-v1.4.1, disk_a.st kept the original non-DOS boot sector header with our
  stage-0 branch placed at offset $18-$1D - right across the BPB's sectors-per-track
  and sides fields ($0060 and $B000).  In 16-bit arithmetic, 96 x 45056 wraps to 0,
  causing DOS Makedisk and other BPB-reading disk writers on PC and Atari ST to fail
  with a Divide Error (or reject the image as corrupt).
* v1.4.2 moves the stage-0 branch past the BPB ($1E) and puts a valid Atari ST
  800 KB BPB (512 B/sector, 10 sectors/track, 2 sides, 1600 total sectors) in
  disk_a.st, while preserving the 'MOON' signature at offset 2 that the game checks.
* v1.4.2 also ships uncompressed .msa copies (disk_a.msa and disk_b.msa).

WHY disk_b.st STILL HAS NO BOOT SECTOR / BPB
* Disk 2 is a raw Pack-Ice data stream ("Ice!" at byte 0), identical to the original
  Millennium Disk 2.  It has no boot sector and no BPB by design, and cannot be given
  one because the game's Disk 2 identity check reads the raw bytes at offset $0E-$11
  ($66055505).
* If a disk writer complains about a missing/invalid boot sector on disk_b.st, either
  use disk_b.msa (whose MSA header carries the 80 x 2 x 10 geometry explicitly) or
  force raw 80 x 2 x 10 mode in your writer.

RECOMMENDED WRITERS
* PC / DOS:
    Makedisk (DOS):        makedisk /write disk_a.st /auto   (or use disk_a.msa / disk_b.msa)
    FloImg (Windows):      open disk_a.msa / disk_b.msa (or .st in 80x2x10 mode)
    Greaseweazle:          gw write --format=atarist.800 disk_a.st
                           gw write --format=atarist.800 disk_b.st
    HxC floppy emulator:   load disk_a.msa / disk_b.msa (or .st; 80 x 2 x 10)
* Atari ST (real hardware):
    JayMSA / MSA Converter / Floppy Image / FastCopy Pro (with .msa):
      write disk_a.msa and disk_b.msa directly - the MSA header sets 10 sectors/track,
      2 sides, tracks 0-79 automatically.

BOOTING & PLAYING
1. Put Disk 1 in drive A: and power on / reset (1 MB RAM or more; 2 MB+ for F10
   campaigns).
2. At the "Insert Disk 2 and press a key." prompt, either swap Disk 2 into drive A:
   and press a key, or have Disk 2 in drive B: (with Disk 2 already in B: from
   power-on, the prompt is skipped automatically).
3. On a Mega STE, test at 8 MHz with cache off first (the original game's custom FDC
   driver uses fixed 8 MHz delay loops).
